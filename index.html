📖 How to Play Sister Sneak

WASD / Arrows / Touch Move your sister around the 3-floor mansion.

⚡ Character Power (Q / Space) Trigger your sister's special ability (Stealth, Super Dash, Artistic Flow, Ladli Card, Rapid Saboteur)!
Innocent Goal 😇 Finish household chores to reach 100% Cleanliness, or unmask the secret Imposter in emergency meetings!
