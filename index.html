📖 How to Play Sister Sneak

WASD / Arrows / Joystick Move your sister around the 3-floor mansion.
E / Action Button Interact with chores, staircases, and fuse boxes.
⚡ Character Power (Q / Space) Trigger your sister's special ability (Stealth, Super Dash, Artistic Flow, Ladli Card, Rapid Saboteur)!
Innocent Goal 😇 Finish household chores on your task list to reach 100% Cleanliness, or unmask the secret Prankster in emergency meetings!
Prankster Goal 😈 Trigger universal sabotages (Blackout, Door Kundi) and frame other sisters during Emergency Meetings without getting caught!
